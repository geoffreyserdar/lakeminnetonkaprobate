You can keep it in the family.

An inherited home does not have to become a listing tomorrow. If selling feels wrong right now, pause is a real option: lease it, hold it, or take the next step only when the family is ready.

Pause without pressure

Many executors and siblings are not choosing between “sell” and “fail.” They are choosing timing. Holding or leasing can keep the house in the family while probate and emotions settle.

What I help with

Clear next steps on the property side: whether leasing makes sense, what a hold period costs in plain terms, and when a sale would actually serve the estate. I work alongside your attorney. I do not replace them.
